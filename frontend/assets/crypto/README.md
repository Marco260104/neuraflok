## Cryptocurrency marks

The SVG marks in this folder were downloaded from the Simple Icons CDN and are kept as the upstream single-colour artwork. Simple Icons' repository is generally published under CC0; individual icons may carry their own terms. The Monero mark (`xmr.svg`) is identified by upstream as CC BY-SA 4.0 and sourced from the [Monero press kit](https://www.getmonero.org/press-kit). Keep that mark under CC BY-SA 4.0 and retain this attribution when redistributing it.

Upstream source records:

- Bitcoin: https://bitcoin.org
- Ethereum: https://ethereum.org/en/assets/
- Tether: https://tether.to/branding/
- Litecoin: https://litecoin-foundation.org/litecoin-branding-guidelines/
- Dogecoin: https://cryptologos.cc/dogecoin
- Solana: https://solana.com/branding
- XRP: https://xrpl.org
- Zcash: https://z.cash
- Monero: https://www.getmonero.org/press-kit

HYPE and TRX use ticker monograms because Simple Icons did not provide matching entries at download time. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
